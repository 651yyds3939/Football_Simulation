What does this scene show?
  • Displays positions, decision labels, actions and match events recorded from the current Python 2D model. Playback speed changes viewing speed only.
  • Motion and contact use a simplified disc model. NAO joints, balance, falls, camera localisation and ball flight are not simulated.
  • Positions and headings are discrete samples. No movement is interpolated across halftime placements or referee resets. A behaviour label does not prove skill success.
  • The scorebar shows the policy used by each team. Halftime formation placement is a test fixture.
  • This viewer replays a generated match. Players cannot be dragged, and the recorded outcome cannot be changed. Generate a new scene after modifying a policy.
